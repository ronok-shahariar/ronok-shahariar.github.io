## Part 1 Summary: High-Speed RF Ingress

The front-end acquisition layer is executed via Satellite Flex Compute FPGA modules, which digitize wideband RF downlinks and encapsulate raw I/Q samples into high-speed Ethernet streams.

### Key Engineering Functions

* **High-Speed Optical Links:** Captures multi-carrier and wideband data, streaming up to 25 Gbps per SDR channel directly into the capture server NICs.
* **Packet Header Instrumentation:** Injects deterministic hardware timestamps and sequence counters into packet headers to ensure zero-loss auditing and time alignment across multiple spatial channels.
* **Dynamic RF Bandwidth Scaling:** Supports 4T4R, 3T3R, and Ultra-Wideband modes (2 GHz and 4 GHz), enabling scalable ingestion up to a maximum aggregate rate of 5.902 GHz.
