About

Engineering systems from the hardware layer to high-performance software.

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) . My interests sit at the intersection of hardware and software, particularly high-speed networking, embedded systems, computer architecture, FPGA-based systems, and hardware verification.

I enjoy understanding systems at a low level and turning that understanding into practical implementations that improve performance, reliability, and efficiency.
