Hi, I'm **Md. Shahariar Hassan Ronok**, an Electrical and Computer Engineering graduate from <a href="https://www.ruet.ac.bd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)</a>.

I enjoy working on problems where hardware and software come together. I am especially interested in high-speed networking, computer architecture, embedded systems, digital hardware, and hardware verification.

What I enjoy most is understanding how a system works at a lower level and then finding practical ways to make it faster, more reliable, and more efficient. I also enjoy building and testing real systems rather than working only with theory.

## Research Interests

- **High-Speed Networking:**  
  I work with <a href="https://www.dpdk.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Data Plane Development Kit (DPDK)</a> for high-speed packet processing, along with <a href="https://docs.kernel.org/admin-guide/mm/hugetlbpage.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">HugePages</a>, Central Processing Unit (CPU) core isolation, zero-copy packet processing, and low-latency data pipelines.

- **Computer Architecture & Digital Systems:**  
  I am interested in processor design and digital systems, especially <a href="https://riscv.org/specifications/ratified/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RISC-V</a> based processors. My work includes pipelined processor design, <a href="https://github.com/YosysHQ/picorv32" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PicoRV32</a>, Read-After-Write (RAW) hazard handling, data forwarding, and implementation on a Field-Programmable Gate Array (FPGA).

- **FPGA Development:**  
  I have experience working with Field-Programmable Gate Array (FPGA) based digital designs, including hardware implementation and testing on platforms such as the **Xilinx Artix-7**.

- **Wireless Communication Security:**  
  My research also covers physical-layer security in wireless communication systems. I have studied secrecy performance in networks containing randomly distributed eavesdroppers using mathematical models such as the Poisson Point Process (PPP).

- **Hardware Verification:**  
  I use Python-based verification tools such as <a href="https://docs.cocotb.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cocotb</a> and <a href="https://verilator.org/guide/latest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Verilator</a> to test digital hardware designs. I am also interested in automated regression testing and Continuous Integration / Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) workflows for hardware projects.
