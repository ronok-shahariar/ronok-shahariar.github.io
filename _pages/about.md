I am an electrical and computer engineering researcher focused on solving bottlenecks across the hardware-software boundary. My work centers on high-throughput, deterministic low-latency systems for next-generation network infrastructure and edge computing.

### Primary Research Interests
* **High-Speed Networking & Kernel Bypass:** Zero-copy packet streaming via DPDK, HugePages allocation, CPU core isolation, and lockless memory pipelines.
* **Microarchitecture & Digital Systems:** Pipelined datapath implementation (RISC-V/PicoRV32), custom hazard mitigation, and FPGA hardware prototyping (Xilinx Artix-7).
* **Physical-Layer Wireless Security:** Evaluating secrecy outage probability and secrecy rate bounds over stochastic channel models using Poisson point process (PPP) geometry.
* **Hardware Verification:** Cycle-accurate Python-based functional co-simulation with Cocotb, Verilator, and automated CI/CD regression suites.
